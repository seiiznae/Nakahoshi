Your gateway toNakaHoshi.

NakaHoshi is a community hub and catalog for donghua (Chinese animation) with Indonesian subtitles, hosted on Telegram. It has attracted over 25,000 public members, alongside a number of paid subscribers supported by a membership system and a dedicated Mini App. We are currently conducting initial experiments toward a web-based version while simultaneously refining the Mini App—please continue exploring our catalog via the NakaHoshi Mini App on Telegram.
